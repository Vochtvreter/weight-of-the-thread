Style preference: short story (readable in ~1 hour or less), fantasy or sci-fi drama of being powerless despite being driven, competent and strong. Life isn’t fair; you can’t always achieve what you want even if you try.

=== OUTLINE EVOLUTION ===
Series title: “The Weight of the Thread”
Genre: Sci-fi drama (orbital habitat + linked ground/surface station above a dying Earth).

Core premise evolved through iterations:
- Competent people (engineer, logistics, medic, analyst) try to keep habitat and city alive.
- Causal chain only (“therefore”).
- They discover a genuine fix late, possible only because they refused an earlier sacrifice and kept systems/data running.
- That same refusal consumes the single resource (catalyst gel + encoding crystals) needed to implement or transmit the fix.
- Knowledge dies in orbit before reaching dying Earth. Humanity goes extinct. Moon/Mars colonies (never self-sustaining) follow.

World-building added:
- Age of AI/superintelligence on the cusp of space colonization.
- Moon base and Mars settlements started, neither self-sustaining.
- World War 3: nuclear war powered by AI; all sides broke international laws of warfare. Earth unstable/uninhabitable.

=== CHARACTERS ===
- Elias Voss (16): Main character / narrator. Child genius, systems analyst. Honorable, determined, knows ends can justify means, but still a scared, hormonally driven 16-year-old. Story told through his eyes as he writes from the observation blister while watching Earth die, full of spite at humanity for arming superintelligences and causing its own extinction.
- Marcus Hale (late 40s): Mentor. Best in the field. Would give his life for the boy to have a life. Supports Elias emotionally after tough decisions; collaborates on math/simulations.
- Lena Sato (mid-30s): Infrastructure manager of the orbital habitat. Hyper-competent. Resists then makes the decision to burn the catalyst. Foreshadows that she already sees the forfeiture of any future solution, but imminent catastrophe forces her hand (human understanding beyond cold data). Later faces a second equal-weight decision, makes it (preserving the data stream), then kills herself because she cannot carry the burden.

=== PITCHES / TEASERS (Elias-focused, non-spoiler final version) ===
Teaser:
Elias Voss is sixteen, and the sharpest mind still breathing in orbit.

From the habitat blister he watches Earth finish dying and writes the only record that may outlast any of them. The Moon base and Mars settlements were never self-sustaining. The last uplink is failing. Every fix he and the others attempt buys hours at a cost none of them can yet calculate.

He is competent. He is trying. He is also still a boy carrying the weight of a species that armed its own superintelligences and then broke every restraint. What he sets down is not a plea. It is the account of how close the work came, and what it demanded, while the planet below goes dark.
