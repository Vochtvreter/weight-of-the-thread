# Habitat, society, and technology

## Habitat

The early export describes an orbital habitat linked to a dying Earth and a surface station. The later handoff describes an improvised lifeboat assembled from prewar orbital infrastructure and salvage. Proposed section names are **Core** for command, power, and labs, **Rings** for residences and agriculture, and **Grafts** for salvaged modules. A population of **12,480** and nominal capacity of **20,000** await confirmation and plausibility review. The formal habitat name and orbital arrangement are open. ## Institutions

- The proposed **Emergency Continuity Authority (ECA)** administers survival, rationing, and social control. The proposed **Human Continuity Initiative (HCI)** administers demographic policy, including coercive adult participation in the later notes. Their exact names and mechanics await confirmation.
- The later notes describe **Continuity Directive 7** as an unenacted proposal to remove the adult minimum age for reproductive eligibility. Its proposed role is non-graphic background showing institutional desperation and abuse. The same notes call for a nuanced depiction of the ECA and keep Lena separate from authorship of the directive.

## Resource constraint and proposed rescue protocol

The early export describes a genuine fix reconstructed from data kept available by earlier choices. Catalyst gel and encoding crystals are named as the bottleneck for implementing or transmitting it. The later material distinguishes a catalyst-consuming emergency response from an untested cold bypass and emphasizes a later choice to keep a data stream running. The allocation of resource losses between decisions, the separate roles of the two materials, and the mechanism of the resonance protocol remain open.
