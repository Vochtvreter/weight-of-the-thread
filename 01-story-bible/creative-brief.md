# Creative brief

## Direct author direction in the export

Write a short story readable in about an hour or less, with a start-to-finish plot, few loose ends, and an interesting twist. Connect events through cause and consequence. Let readers infer the inevitability of the result. The author describes capable, driven people who remain powerless to secure the outcome they want, and initially leaves room for either fantasy or science-fiction drama. [Original transcript, Initial Request](../07-archive/imports/codex-prompt-4/sources/original_export/transcript.txt)

## Working direction in the source pack

- The developed material uses the title *The Weight of the Thread* and takes the form of an orbital science-fiction tragedy. A genuine solution becomes available after the decisions that make it unusable. Humanity goes extinct and the dependent Moon and Mars settlements perish. [Original transcript, Outline Evolution](../07-archive/imports/codex-prompt-4/sources/original_export/transcript.txt)
- Later notes give a target of approximately 6,000–9,000 words, emotionally restrained narration, and a tragic ending with no hidden rescue. The source pack reports one uncertain untaken choice whose outcome can never be known. The early wording on inevitability and the later counterfactual both need review. [Later handoff, Creative Brief](../07-archive/imports/codex-prompt-4/handoff/CONVERSATION_HANDOFF.md) · [Canon review](../07-archive/imports/codex-prompt-4/handoff/CANON_REVIEW.md)
- Keep the internal outline and the public pitch distinct. The later notes call for a spoiler-free synopsis. The archived teaser, despite its label, says Elias watches Earth finish dying and describes a failing uplink. Its suitability for public use remains open.
