# Imported research questions

The handoff supplies research topics. The package supplies no scientific references or completed plausibility studies.

- **Long-term habitat survival.** What would sustain the proposed population for roughly three decades? Review closed-loop food, water, oxygen, energy, heat rejection, medicine, manufacturing, and spare parts.
- **Physical layout and dependencies.** What orbital arrangement and ground links are plausible for a salvaged habitat? How would the non-self-sustaining Moon and Mars settlements depend on Earth or orbit, and when would those links fail?
- **Earth's final failure.** What mechanism could make the late cascade scientifically defensible? Clarify what is habitable or functional after the war and what the final cascade changes.
- **The valve, resources, and protocol.** What do catalyst gel and encoding crystals do, which decisions consume them, why can they neither be replaced nor substituted, and why does the reconstructed protocol need them? What is known about the cold bypass at the decision point?
