## Draft statuses

- **NOT STARTED** means the chapter or scene has a slot in the index.
- **OUTLINED** means beats exist and the manuscript text has yet to be drafted.
- **FIRST DRAFT** means an initial draft is available for review.
- **REVISING** means the draft is being revised.
- **APPROVED** means the author has approved this draft for the manuscript. Canon approval follows the [separate workflow](../AGENTS.md#propose-review-and-apply-changes).
