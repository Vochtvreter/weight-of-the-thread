# Ava standalone holographic wardrobe prompts · v29

Five provisional wardrobe tests generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-11-26. Each illustration used only its own prompt. No image was attached to any generation.

The [v30 follow-up](prompt-v30.md) tested more specific wording for Ava's hip proportions. For new generations, use the [current body description in the visual style guide](../../visual-style-and-character-prompts.md#character-design-notes). The prompts below record the wording used for the v29 renders.

## Prismatic ribbon teddy

[Illustration](ava-prismatic-ribbon-v29.png)

```text
Paint a naturalistic gouache fashion portrait of Ava, a nineteen-year-old adult holographic woman who appears physically present. She has light skin with warm freckles over her nose and cheeks, bright green almond-shaped eyes, brown brows with a soft arch, smooth dimple-free cheeks, and a slim jaw that tapers to a small rounded chin. Her sandy beige blonde hair has slightly darker roots, a soft side part, a long sweep across her forehead and a smaller section tucked behind her left ear. A loose three-strand braid rests over her left shoulder. She wears dusty rose lipstick, brown mascara, and black eyeliner with a small upward flick. She is slightly tall and fine-framed with a lightly athletic build, slender shoulders, a gently tapered waist, relatively narrow hips, a slightly longer torso and proportionally shorter lower legs.

At the foot of the berth in Elias’s quarters, Ava wears an opaque ink-blue one-shoulder teddy formed from overlapping diagonal satin panels. Fine projected threads create narrow bands of cyan and lavender iridescence where the panels turn toward the light. The bands follow the weave and gathers like threads in the garment. She stands at ease, facing the viewer at a shallow angle, with her left foot half a step forward and both feet flat. Keep a small natural gap between her upper thighs. Her left hand rests beside her left hip and her right hand hangs by her other side. She lifts one eyebrow and meets our eyes with a daring half-smile.

Elias’s private cabin is a small room aboard an orbital habitat, with a fixed berth, matte slate bulkheads, a low amber reading lamp, and cool blue light from a dark porthole. Ava is the only figure. Her body and clothing are projected light. A thin blue fringe at one seam reveals the hologram and the surrounding objects stay still. Keep the face distinct and normally lit, the clothing fully opaque where it covers intimate areas, and the digital patterns local to the fabrics. Natural camera perspective at eye level, one vertical 2:3 full-figure composition, anatomically natural hands and limbs, matte gouache with visible fine paper grain, no lettering.
```
