# Ava appearance and portrait prompt

## Reusable appearance block

Use this block in any text-only Ava prompt. For a scene, follow it with the wardrobe, pose, expression, and setting. The [Story Bible](../../01-story-bible/elias-marcus-ai-relationship.md#avas-appearance-to-elias) records the approved appearance, with [v15](../../05-artwork/concept-art/ava/ava-concept-v15.png) for her face and hair and [v28](../../05-artwork/concept-art/ava/ava-plum-lace-v28.png) for her proportions.

```text
Ava is a holographic woman who appears about nineteen. She has light skin and fine warm freckles across her nose and smooth, dimple-free cheeks. Her bright green, almond-shaped eyes sit under gently arched brown brows. Her nose has a softly rounded tip and her slim jaw tapers to a small rounded chin. Her sandy beige blonde hair has slightly darker roots and a soft side part. A loose sweep frames one side of her face, with a few wisps around the forehead. She tucks the shorter section behind her left ear and wears a loose three-strand braid over her left shoulder.

Ava is slightly tall and fine-framed, with slender shoulders and a softly tapered waist. Seen from the front, her slender hips are a little narrower than her shoulder span. The waist flows into a shallow curve at the hips, then smoothly into relatively straight outer upper thighs. Her torso is slightly longer relative to her legs, and her lower legs are proportionally shorter.
```

## Standalone portrait prompt

Paste the appearance block and the portrait direction below in order as one prompt. [Current portrait](portrait.png) · [Historical v15 generation prompt](../../05-artwork/concept-art/ava/prompt-v15.md)

```text
Paint a vertical 2:3 upper-body portrait at eye level aboard an orbital habitat. Ava faces us with a relaxed, attentive expression. Show her whole braid over a dusty rose knit sweater with an ivory collar. She wears dusty rose lipstick, brown mascara, and cocoa upper eyeliner with a small outer flick. Fine amber threads follow the knit, with a faint cool projection fringe at one cuff. The projected figure makes no physical contact with the room. Use naturalistic matte gouache, fine paper grain, warm practical light, and a muted slate station wall.
```
