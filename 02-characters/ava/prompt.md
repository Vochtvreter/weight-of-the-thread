## Standalone prompt

```text
Paint Ava, a nineteen-year-old holographic woman aboard an orbital habitat. She has light skin and fine warm freckles concentrated across her nose and cheeks. Her bright green almond-shaped eyes sit beneath gently arched brown brows. Her nose has a softly rounded tip, her cheeks are smooth and dimple-free, and her slim jaw tapers to a small rounded chin. Her sandy beige blonde hair has slightly darker roots. A soft side part lets a loose sweep fall over the viewer-left side of her forehead. The shorter section is tucked behind the visible ear on the viewer’s right, with a loose three-strand braid falling over that same shoulder. Her makeup is dusty rose lipstick, brown mascara, and upper eyeliner with a small outer flick.

Ava is slightly tall and fine-framed, with slender shoulders and a gently tapered waist. Seen from the front, her hips are a little narrower than her shoulder span. Her waist flows into a shallow outward curve at the hips and then into relatively straight outer upper thighs. Her torso is slightly longer and her lower legs are proportionally shorter.

Paint a vertical 2:3 upper-body portrait at eye level. Ava faces us with a relaxed, attentive expression. Show her whole braid over a dusty rose knit sweater with an ivory collar. Fine amber threads follow the knit, with a faint cool projection fringe at one cuff. The projected figure makes no physical contact with the room. Use naturalistic matte gouache, fine paper grain, warm practical light, and a muted slate station wall.
```
