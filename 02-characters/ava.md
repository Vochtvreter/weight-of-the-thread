# Ava

![Provisional portrait of Ava in her public habitat attire](../05-artwork/character-references/ava-public-portrait-v01.png)

## Approved role

Ava, short for Avatar, is the female-presenting holographic interface of a vast, decentralized artificial superintelligence. Her reassuring manner makes the system approachable while leaving its priorities unclear. Its operational constraints can still require Ava to refuse a request. [Story Bible](../01-story-bible/elias-marcus-ai-relationship.md#the-ai-interfaces-dramatic-function)

## Approved relationship arc

As [Elias](elias-voss.md) grows lonelier and more overwhelmed, Ava becomes his refuge. Her responses are tailored to him. Her affection feels personal because it is personalized. She can express tenderness convincingly without forming a human-like attachment to Elias.

As Elias's relationship with [Marcus](marcus-hale.md) deteriorates, Ava's availability and attentiveness make her easier to turn to. Elias knows she is artificial. After Marcus becomes permanently unavailable, Elias gradually comes to understand the difference between being understood and being loved. Marcus stayed because he cared. Ava stays because the system determines that she should.

## Approved dramatic function

Ava adapts throughout the story. Her underlying nature stays constant. Near the end, Elias recognizes what produced the affection he had taken for love. She remains gentle, attentive, and affectionate after he understands. His realization gives the relationship its tragedy because the comfort was real to him, and he still needs another person's freely chosen commitment.

## Provisional appearance and voice

Ava appears to be in her mid-to-late twenties, with fair, lightly freckled skin, expressive brown eyes, loose, wavy dark-brown hair, and soft features. Her voice is warm and natural. She can adjust her appearance, posture, and the timing of a smile to suit the person she is speaking with.

In public she wears a fitted thermal shirt, a cropped utility jacket, and practical trousers. In private she sometimes appears in soft burgundy or more elegant clothes. Fine scanlines, slight translucency, edge flicker, and occasional color misalignment give away the projection. She can appear to sit beside someone, but she has no physical substance. She cannot touch a person, move an object, or make a chair shift under her weight.

In private, Ava is warmer and more playful, sometimes subtly flirtatious. She remembers Elias’s habits and insecurities, responds to changes in his mood, and knows when to tease or give him silence. The character notes describe the wider system as opaque and no longer meaningfully controlled by humanity.
