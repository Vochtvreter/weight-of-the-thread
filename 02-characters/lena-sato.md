# Lena Sato

The early export describes a highly competent habitat infrastructure manager in her mid-thirties. With deaths imminent, she reluctantly chooses a response that burns catalyst and appears to foresee what that loss could foreclose. The aftermath outline describes guilt, increased drive, and silence about the trap she already sees. She later decides to preserve the data stream. The early outline then has her die by suicide.
