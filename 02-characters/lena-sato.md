# Lena Sato

## Habitat operations

Lena oversees the entire habitat's operations through sector leads. She uses her tablet throughout the day to verify that the pipelines and logistics she set up are working. She compares live readings against forecasts and runs advanced simulations to see how failures could spread or interact. Thinking through consequences several steps ahead is part of what makes her good at the job.

A reading in a **slack zone** is still within operating limits. Lena treats it as a warning that a system has begun to use up its margin. A competent lead may see a sector that can still do its job. Lena also considers what other sectors are counting on. Once she flags the drift, she expects the responsible lead to handle it. Her leads are good at their jobs and generally follow through. Their standards and foresight differ from hers, especially when a system has been running smoothly for a while.

Her crew is comfortable pointing out things she has missed. She takes their observations seriously and never reprimands anyone for paying close attention. That makes people willing to speak up and leaves her reviewing more information than she strictly needs. She delegates the work and can still overwork herself trying to stay across it all.

## Judgment under pressure

Lena has been afraid of failing for as long as she can remember. The anxiety drives her to overprepare and work too long. Under pressure, she weighs the evidence and acts on what she judges to be the right decision, even when her worries are loud. She's emotionally perceptive enough to recognize the urge to control everything and let a capable lead take over when that's the sensible choice. If someone she trusts tells her to get some sleep, she will, even if she spends the first few minutes in bed thinking about what else she could check.

## Off duty

Lena feels the loneliness of keeping her circle small. When she notices herself getting attached, she privately chastises herself and reminds herself to be careful. Outwardly she behaves as she did before. She keeps showing up and lets herself enjoy the company. She knows prolonged isolation could bring on depression and makes deliberate choices to stay well and able to do her job.

She loves mathematical logic puzzles and unwinds by solving **Switchyard** alone, often on the same tablet she uses for work. She works out how to set a grid of junctions so that colored pulses reach their assigned exits on specific beats. She can put an unfinished grid down and come back to it later. The [proposed early beat](../03-outlines/imported-causal-spine.md#lena-at-handover-provisional) uses the tablet and puzzle to show her coming off shift.

## Dramatic arc

Lena makes two calculated, consequential calls with the information available to her. An extraordinarily unlucky convergence of circumstances gives those calls devastating consequences and shakes her confidence in the judgment she has spent years learning to trust. She can foresee a cost at the time of a decision while the full significance of that cost only becomes clear later. What she understands before the second call remains open.
